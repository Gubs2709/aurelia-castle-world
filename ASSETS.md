# Asset provenance

- **Knight:** Quaternius, “Animated Knight Character”, CC0 (public domain). Downloaded from the Three.js manual example mirror. Local files: `public/models/knight.gltf` and `public/models/KnightCharacter.bin`. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/lowpoly-animated-knight . Mirror: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/manual/examples/resources/models/knight . The original clip names are retained; walking uses the run cycle at reduced speed, and jumping adds a procedural airborne pose.
- **Environment:** Original procedural geometry, materials, sign textures, dragon, vegetation, and UI created for this project. No remotely hosted textures, fonts, or runtime asset services are used.
- **Reference JPEGs:** Supplied by the user. Kept intact in the project root as visual references; not embedded in the distributed game. Ownership and redistribution rights have not been asserted.
- **Libraries:** Three.js (MIT), Rapier (Apache-2.0), and Vite/TypeScript tooling retain their upstream licenses in their packages.
