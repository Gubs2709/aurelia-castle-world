# Aurelia art direction

The supplied 19 images remain the visual target. The current Unity scene is an implementation pass toward that target, not proof of equivalent fidelity.

## Additional references

- **Wuthering Waves:** Kuro's [official developer interview](https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/exploring-the-post-apocalyptic-charm-of-asg-open-worlds-in-wuthering-waves) discusses complementary colour, controlled grading, a stylized sky/daylight system, and foliage representations across distance. Applied here through warm village/cool slate contrast, a procedural sky, atmospheric mountain layers, and distinct grove colours. The small project does not implement Kuro's proprietary rendering pipeline.
- **The Beginning After the End:** [official Tapas series](https://tapas.io/series/tbate-comic/info). A fantasy-adventure reference for the journey from a human-scale settlement toward a much larger magical world. This is our interpretation for Aurelia's destination-driven composition, not a claim about an exact scene reproduced from the comic.
- **Tower of God:** [official WEBTOON series](https://www.webtoons.com/en/fantasy/tower-of-god/list?title_no=95). A reference for the sense of ascent and imposing destinations. Aurelia uses its own castle and viaduct geometry.

No panels, game textures, copyrighted characters, or proprietary models from these references are embedded in the game.

## Composition rules for further work

1. The castle silhouette should remain readable from the main route. Foreground foliage frames it; it must not obscure the gate.
2. Ground-level scenes need three layers: detailed nearby foliage or architecture, a traversable middle distance, and a clear distant destination.
3. Warm amber and burgundy identify the village. Ivory and desaturated blue identify the castle. Teal, muted lavender, and scattered luminous accents identify the grove.
4. Height must serve the view and the route. Keep ramps walkable and do not substitute a teleport for a broken walking approach.
5. Add local detail where the traveller can approach it. Repeated large flat walls, empty grass expanses, uniform trees, and isolated props are the next art problems to address.

## Honest quality bar

The current pass adds more geometry, textured foliage, rendered water, architectural trim, gardens, coloured atmosphere, and an armoured traveller. It still needs more bespoke modular architecture, stronger terrain erosion and biome transitions, refined materials, animated waterfall meshes/foam, character animation polish, and a stronger creature model to approach the illustrations or a commercial anime open world. These are production/art tasks; changing engines alone does not supply them.
