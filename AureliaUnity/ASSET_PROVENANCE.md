# Asset provenance

## Quaternius — Ultimate Stylized Nature

Official source: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatestylizednature.html

CC0 1.0. The official page links to the Google Drive folder used for downloads. Selected FBX trees, bushes, grass, flowers and textures are in `Assets/Art/StylizedNature`. The source license is included in that directory, together with `provenance.json`, which records file IDs, downloaded byte sizes and SHA-256 hashes. The downloaded license text has a reused "Ultimate Platformer Pack" heading; both its CC0 declaration and the official nature-pack page state CC0. Source textures have been imported with a 1024 px maximum to limit GPU memory.

## Kenney — Fantasy Town Kit 2.0

Official source: https://kenney.nl/assets/fantasy-town-kit

CC0. `Assets/Art/FantasyTown/LICENSE.txt` preserves the included license. Download: https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/fantasy-town-kit/efe948d309-1754222374/kenney_fantasy-town-kit_2.0.zip . Market stalls, cart and supporting props are used. Main castle and houses are custom generated geometry.

## Kenney — Nature Kit

Official source: https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit

CC0. `Assets/Art/Nature/LICENSE.txt` preserves the included license. Download: https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/nature-kit/37ac38a37b-1677698939/kenney_nature-kit.zip . Rocks, mushrooms and canoe are used. Basic pack trees were replaced in the world by textured Quaternius trees.

## Quaternius — Animated Knight

The original browser project's local knight model was imported using Unity glTFast 6.20.0. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/lowpoly-animated-knight . CC0, as documented in the existing parent `ASSETS.md`. Local source files remain `public/models/knight.gltf` and `KnightCharacter.bin`. Idle and run animations are connected through an Animator controller. Armour, shield, sheathed sword, and cloak are custom geometry. There is no sword combat.

## Custom content and reference images

Terrain, castle, town-house structures, bridge, sky and water shaders, flowers, guardian, ambient dragon, costume additions, and interface were authored for this project. The 19 supplied reference JPEGs remain untouched in the parent folder and are not embedded in the built game.

Wuthering Waves, open-world games, and adventure manhwa are art-direction references only. Their proprietary models, textures, music, and characters were not imported.
